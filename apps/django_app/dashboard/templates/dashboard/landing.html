The Kenya SME Intelligence Platform

A flagship, production-oriented portfolio system demonstrating how modern Agentic AI, deterministic financial calculations, and explainable ML empower small and medium enterprises in Kenya.

LangGraph Multi-Agent Orchestration

9 specialized AI agents (Financial, Credit Risk, Transaction, Anomaly, Invoice, Reporting, and RAG) orchestrated by a supervisor state machine to deliver grounded, evidence-backed answers.

Deterministic Mathematical Grounding

Zero LLM arithmetic hallucination. Ratios, P&L statements, balance sheets, and turnover metrics are calculated directly via Python/PostgreSQL engines before being summarized by the AI.

Explainable Credit Risk Scorecard

L2-regularised logistic regression scorecard trained on 36-month SME profiles (AUC 1.000 / 0.930), returning exact additive log-odds reason codes for transparent credit committee decisions.

Multi-Format RAG Knowledge Engine

Ingests and chunks PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint decks, and policy markdowns, enabling business owners to query contractual terms and lending policies in natural language.

Surveillance & Anomaly Detection

Monitors 540,000+ longitudinal transaction records across M-Pesa and banking rails, applying Z-score and off-hours statistical filters to detect fraudulent or erratic spending.

Automated Executive Reporting

One-click generation and download of 5-slide Executive PowerPoint decks (.pptx) and multi-tab Excel financial models (.xlsx) for board and loan reviews.
