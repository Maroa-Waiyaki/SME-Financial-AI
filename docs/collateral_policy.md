# Kenya SME Collateral & Security Policy

## Collateral Requirements

All SME credit facilities above KES 500,000 require registered security. Acceptable collateral forms include:

- **Commercial & Residential Real Estate**: Charge over title deed with minimum 120% forced sale value (FSV) coverage.
- **Motor Vehicles & Equipment**: Maximum age 8 years, comprehensive insurance endorsed to the lender, up to 70% valuation.
- **Cash Margins & Fixed Deposits**: 100% cash-backed lien at agreed interest margin.
- **Chattels & Inventory**: Floating charge over stock for wholesale and retail SMEs with audited inventory records.

## Personal Guarantees

- Directors of limited liability companies must provide joint and several personal guarantees.
- For sole proprietorships, spousal consent is required for matrimonial property charges.

## Valuation & Perfection

- Valuations must be conducted by an approved valuer within the last 12 months.
- Legal charges must be perfected and registered at the relevant lands or companies registry before disbursement.
