# SME Financial Management Guide (Kenya)

## Managing Cash Flow

Cash flow is the net amount of cash and cash-equivalents moving into and out of a business. For Kenyan SMEs, especially those using M-Pesa, it is important to track cash inflows from sales and outflows from expenses daily. A positive cash flow means the business has enough cash to meet its obligations.

## Revenue vs Profit

Revenue is the total amount of money earned from sales. Profit is what remains after all expenses are subtracted. A business can have high revenue and low profit, or even a loss, if expenses are not controlled.

## Common Expense Categories

Common SME expenses in Kenya include rent, salaries, transport, utilities, inventory, marketing, taxes, M-Pesa fees, bank fees, loan repayments, and maintenance.

## M-Pesa Fees

M-Pesa transaction fees reduce the amount received on each sale. Businesses should account for these fees as an expense and consider them when pricing products.

## Outstanding Invoices

An invoice is a request for payment. If an invoice is not paid by its due date, it becomes outstanding. High outstanding invoices reduce cash flow and increase credit risk.
