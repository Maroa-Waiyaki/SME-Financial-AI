# SME Credit Policy Guide

## Risk Factors

Lenders evaluate several factors when assessing an SME:

- Expense-to-revenue ratio: a high ratio means low profit margin.
- Revenue trend: declining revenue is a warning sign.
- Cash flow: negative cash flow indicates difficulty meeting obligations.
- Overdue invoices: high outstanding or overdue invoices suggest weak collections.
- Existing debt: large outstanding loans reduce repayment capacity.

## Probability of Default

The probability of default (PD) estimates the likelihood that a business will fail to repay a loan. PD is usually expressed as a percentage between 0% and 100%.

## Debt Service Coverage

Debt service coverage measures whether a business's cash flow is sufficient to cover loan repayments. A value below 1.0 means cash flow is insufficient.

## High-Risk Signals

Businesses with defaulted loans, multiple negative cash-flow months, or unusually high expenses relative to revenue are typically considered high risk.
