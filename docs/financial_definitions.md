# Financial Definitions

## Revenue

Total cash inflows from sales and other business activities during a period.

## Expenses

Costs incurred while operating the business, including rent, salaries, inventory, fees, and loan repayments.

## Profit

Revenue minus expenses. Also called net income.

## Profit Margin

Profit divided by revenue, expressed as a percentage. It shows how much of each shilling in revenue is profit.

## Cash Flow

Net change in cash over a period. Positive cash flow means cash increased; negative cash flow means cash decreased.

## Net Cash Flow

Total cash inflows minus total cash outflows.

## Outstanding Receivables

Money owed to the business that has not yet been paid. Also called accounts receivable.

## Debt Obligations

The total amount a business still owes on loans and other forms of debt.

## Credit Risk

The risk that a borrower will not repay a loan. It is usually summarized as low, medium, or high.
