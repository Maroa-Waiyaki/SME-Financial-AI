# Loan Policy for Kenyan SMEs

## Eligibility

An SME must demonstrate stable or growing revenue, controlled expenses, and positive cash flow to be considered for a loan.

## Loan Terms

Typical loan terms include an annual interest rate between 12% and 24%, a term of 6 to 36 months, and monthly repayments.

## Default

A loan is in default when the borrower fails to make scheduled repayments. Defaulted loans are recorded and reduce future creditworthiness.

## Approval Process

The platform uses a machine-learning credit-risk model to estimate the probability of default. The output is one input to the lending decision, not a guarantee of approval.
